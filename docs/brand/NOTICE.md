# Brand notice (draft)

**SST** and **Session Stats Tracker** are the names of this project and of its official service.
The SST logo and symbol (`docs/brand/`, `public/brand/`, the app icons and the Open Graph image)
are the project's brand assets.

- The source code is licensed under the [MIT License](../../LICENSE). This notice does not change
  that license.
- How the SST name and logo may be used by forks and third parties is not formally defined yet.
  A brand-use policy will be written, with legal review, **before any paid commercialization**.
- Until then, please don't present a fork or another service as the official SST service, and
  don't imply affiliation with it.
- This notice is not legal advice and makes no claim of registered trademark rights.
